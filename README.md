# Free AI Video Upscaler

A simple, quick and free no-nonsense tool for upscaling video with AI upscaling algorithms right in your browser- no signups, no downloads just choose a video and download your upscaled video after it's done processing.

You can get started at 👉 [Free AI Video Upscaler](https://free.upscaler.video/) 👈
